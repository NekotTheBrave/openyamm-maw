# Superseded terrain experiments cleaned

The 2026-09-10 terrain cleanup removed generated PNG/JPEG trials from `out_terrain_land`, `out_terrain_water`, `out_tile_3x3_esrgan`, `out_repeat_4x4`, and `work/terrain_hd`. These experiments were superseded by the accepted packs under `level_generation/terrain`.

Scripts, model weights, original BMPs, editable sources and metadata remain. The bmodel/sprite outputs and MM6/MM7/MM8 x2 surface-texture packs were not removed. See `level_generation/terrain/CLEANUP.md` and `CLEANUP_REPORT.json` for the retention policy, exact deletion list and verification.
